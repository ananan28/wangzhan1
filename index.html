Investment principles投资原则

What I value before the numbers become obvious. 在数字尚未证明一切之前，我更看重这些。

01

Founder integrity创始人品质

Intellectual honesty, resilience and the ability to attract exceptional people.诚实面对问题，具备韧性，并能吸引优秀人才。

02

Evidence before narrative证据先于故事

Especially in science-led companies, validation matters more than a polished pitch.尤其在科学驱动型公司中，真实验证比漂亮叙事更重要。

03

Scale with substance规模与价值并重

Large markets matter, but only when the product creates durable and defensible value.大市场很重要，但前提是产品能创造长期且可防御的价值。
